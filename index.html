NEON DINER

Game Rules

Five reels. Three rows. Five fixed paylines, all active on every spin. Your total bet is divided equally between the five lines.

Line wins

Matching symbols pay from left to right, starting on the first reel. Seven pays for 2–5 matching symbols; food symbols pay for 3–5.

1 · Middle2 · Top3 · Bottom4 · V5 · ∧

Special symbols

WILD jukebox appears on reels 2, 3 and 4. It substitutes for ordinary symbols, but not Dollar or Star.

Dollar pays for 3–5 symbols anywhere. Star pays for 3–5 symbols on reels 1, 3 and 5. Line and special-symbol awards are added together.

Autoplay and connection

Select 5, 10, 25, 50 or 100 spins. Stop Autoplay allows the current spin to finish and prevents the next spin. Balance updates when a round result is received.

While a round is unresolved, another bet cannot be placed. Reconnection retrieves the pending result. Autoplay stays stopped.

This is a frontend preview with authored outcomes and sample money. Live limits, RTP, outcomes and payouts will come from the existing backend. These rules are a design draft, not a new certification document.
